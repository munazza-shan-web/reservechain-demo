ReserveChain is designed to reconcile approved inventory evidence, token supply and reserve calculations while preserving a historical audit record.

Future Proof-of-Reserves workflow

1. Inventory evidence

Approved unit records and documents enter the registry.

2. Reconciliation

Reserve calculations compare approved asset data with authorized token supply.

3. Attestation history

Discrepancies, attestations and historical reports remain traceable.

Proof-of-Reserves is a planned module only and remains inactive until verified information and final approvals are available.
