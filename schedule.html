Proposed delivery schedule

Milestone-driven implementation.

Indicative production plan. Final dates depend on approved scope, legal inputs, asset evidence, third-party accounts and owner acceptance.

Weeks 1–3

Discovery, UX, architecture

Requirements lock, information architecture, data model, security plan and environments.

Weeks 4–8

Website, CMS, registry

Public site, admin, asset programs, passports, documents, workflow states and audit trail.

Weeks 9–13

Backend, identity, APIs

Authentication, MFA, RBAC, secure uploads, multilingual content and operational APIs.

Weeks 14–18

Smart contracts & testnet

Configurable ERC-20 contracts, tests, deployment scripts, verification and admin procedures.

Weeks 19–24

iOS / Android

Shared APIs, authentication, asset programs, passports, documents, holdings/transactions placeholders and notifications.

Weeks 25–28

Testing, hardening, handover

Security review, accessibility, performance, backups/restore, store testing, manuals, training and transfer.

This schedule is an implementation proposal for planning purposes, not a promise of final launch dates. Production acceptance requires testing, documentation, independent verification and owner approval.
