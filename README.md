# ReserveChain.io  Institutional RWA Platform Prototype

ReserveChain.io is a working prelaunch concept for an institutional Real-World Asset (RWA) tokenization platform focused initially on **ultra-high-purity Copper Powder** and **high-purity Nickel Wire**.

This prototype was developed to demonstrate the proposed user experience, asset-management structure, Digital Asset Passports, verification framework, tokenization architecture, and administrative infrastructure.

## Digital Asset Infrastructure

The prototype demonstrates how ReserveChain could organize industrial assets through structured records covering:

- Asset programs
- Lots and batches
- Containers and coils
- Technical specifications
- Certificates and supporting documents
- Verification status
- Custody and reserve information
- Tokenization status
- Redemption status

All unverified information displayed in this prototype is clearly treated as illustrative or pending.

## Digital Asset Passports

The Digital Asset Passport concept provides a structured interface for connecting physical industrial-metal records with supporting documentation, verification status and future tokenization infrastructure.

No illustrative record should be interpreted as a verified real-world asset record.

## Tokenization

The proposed production architecture is designed to support configurable ERC-20 token infrastructure, including controlled minting and burning, permissions, pause controls, treasury administration and future redemption workflows.

Production smart contracts, token economics and mainnet deployment are outside the scope of this front-end contest prototype and remain subject to final technical, legal and security approval.

## CMS & Administration

The Admin/CMS concept demonstrates how authorized ReserveChain personnel could manage:

- Website content
- Industrial assets
- Digital Asset Passports
- Documents
- Verification information
- Legal notices
- Waitlist registrations
- Tokenization data
- Module visibility
- Publication status

The proposed production system would include role-based permissions, approval workflows and tamper-evident audit logging.
